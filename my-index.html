My Journey

From Langata to Aston Martin My journey towards Formula 1 began as a kid karting in Langata, Kenya. I remember the excitement of getting into the kart, the nerves before a race, and the feeling of wanting to prove myself every time I went onto the track. What started as something I loved quickly became a dream I couldn't let go of. Moving to South Africa was a big step for me. I felt nervous and excited at the same time. The competition was tougher, and there were moments when I doubted myself. But every difficult race pushed me to work harder and believe in myself. Reaching Formula 3 was an emotional moment. Sitting on the grid, hearing the engine and waiting for the lights to go out, I thought about how far I had come. I felt proud, grateful, and hungry for more. Today, I race in F3 with Aston Martin. I know how much work it took to get here, and I don't take it for granted. But the dream is still ahead of me. Formula 1. Every race brings me one step closer.

About me

My name is Giggs Muturi, I’m 20 years old, and I’m passionate about sports, cars, motorsports, and technology. I enjoy basketball and football, and I’m always looking for ways to challenge myself and grow. I’m a co-founder of Tugo Sports, where I’m working to use sports to create opportunities for young people and underfunded schools. My ambition is to become a motorsport driver and a software engineer. I’m currently developing my programming skills and have a solid foundation in Python, with a strong desire to keep learning and building.

Vison Board

My vision is to enter Formula 1 by 2030 and continue developing myself both on and off the track. I want to become a successful software engineer who can use technology to solve real-world problems and create solutions that make a difference. I also want to grow Tugo Sports into an organisation that creates greater opportunities for young people through sport. Along the way, I aim to become skilled in at least three programming languages and keep challenging myself to learn, improve, and turn my ambitions into reality.
